# City & World Generation

## District Zoning
The generator divides the world into structured urban districts:
1. **Commercial / City Center**: Multi-story buildings, supermarkets, gun stores, hospitals, and police stations surrounded by paved asphalt roads, sidewalks, and crosswalks.
2. **Residential District**: Houses, apartments, dormitories, and schools with yards, mailboxes, trash cans, and residential streets.
3. **Industrial & Storage District**: Warehouses, garages, parking lots, gas stations, and dirt/connector roads.
4. **Parks & Forest District**: Dense and sparse forests, dead trees, dense grass, dry grass, sand, and rivers with bridges.

## Tile Types & Gameplay Effects
- **Highway / Major Roads**: Highest speed multiplier (1.2x).
- **Dirt Roads / Sidewalks**: Normal speed multiplier (1.0x).
- **Dense Grass / Sand**: Reduced speed multiplier (0.75x - 0.85x).
- **Dense Forest / Dead Trees**: Reduced speed and vision obstruction.
